*A lone blue cube is sent into outer space, on a quest to document what it sees, until it realizes space is not a friendly place after all...*

***

**Project Neyon** is an action-packed space shooter game written from scratch in Zig.

### Features:
- Fast runs (under 30 minutes)
- 100 handcrafted waves of enemies
- 2 unique boss battles
- 16 weapons to discover

***

The game is in active development, but not yet fully released. If you're interested in becoming a playtester and trying it out however, feel free to join the discord server!
